# Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

**Last updated: May 16, 2026**

## English

**Zhuangzi's Daily ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.**

### Data Collection

This app collects **no personal information**. The app operates entirely on your device:

- All sign content (81 Zhuangzi quotes) is embedded locally in the app bundle.
- Your sign history, daily sign data, and preferences are stored **only on your device** via `UserDefaults`.
- No data is sent to any server, cloud, or third-party service.

### Subscriptions

Subscription purchases are processed entirely by Apple's StoreKit framework and App Store. We do not receive or store any payment information. Subscription status is checked locally on your device.

### Third-Party Services

This app uses **no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services**. There are no SDKs that collect data.

### Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, deleting the app will remove all associated data. You can also reset data via the app's settings.

### Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at **516459463@qq.com**.

## 中文

**庄子的每日签（以下简称"我们"）尊重您的隐私。本应用不收集、存储或传输任何个人数据。**

### 数据收集

本应用**不收集任何个人信息**。所有操作均在你的设备本地完成：

- 全部 81 条庄子签数据内置于应用包中，无需联网加载。
- 签到历史、每日签数据、偏好设置仅存储在设备本地（通过 `UserDefaults`）。
- 没有任何数据被发送到服务器、云端或第三方服务。

### 订阅

订阅购买完全由 Apple 的 StoreKit 框架和 App Store 处理。我们不会收到或存储任何支付信息。订阅状态仅在设备本地检查。

### 第三方服务

本应用**不使用任何第三方分析、广告或跟踪服务**。没有集成任何数据收集 SDK。

### 数据删除

所有数据均存储在设备本地。删除应用即可清除所有关联数据。你也可以在应用设置中重置数据。
